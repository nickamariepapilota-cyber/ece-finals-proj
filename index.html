OUR PURPOSE

Protecting what makes
our islands alive.

The Visayas is home to remarkable wildlife found nowhere else on Earth. Yet habitat loss, hunting, and human activities threaten the survival of many native species.

WildVisayas is an educational initiative dedicated to raising awareness, encouraging conservation, and inspiring communities to protect our natural heritage.

✳

Our Mission

To inspire a generation that understands, values, and protects the endangered wildlife of the Visayan islands.
